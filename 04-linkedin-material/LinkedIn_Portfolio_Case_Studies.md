## Project 1 — Lightspeed Retail X-Series Inventory Cleanup & Upload Preparation

### LinkedIn caption

I built a complete inventory-cleaning and upload-preparation workflow for Lightspeed Retail X-Series.

The challenge was to turn inconsistent source inventory into a structured, import-ready product file while keeping every correction visible and auditable.

What I delivered:

- Cleaned and standardized 30 realistic product records
- Resolved duplicate and invalid SKU formats
- Corrected negative stock and pricing conflicts
- Prepared an upload-ready Lightspeed X-Series CSV
- Built a QC dashboard with pass/fail validation controls
- Added a field guide and a row-level cleaning log
- Included 300 prepared rows for scaling the template

Result: a reusable retail data-cleaning system that reduces import risk and gives the store owner a clear review process before upload.

Portfolio note: This is a self-initiated demonstration project using fictional product data. The workflow and deliverables are fully functional.

### LinkedIn caption

This portfolio system covers five sample properties and turns day-to-day hosting activity into a simple operating dashboard.

What I delivered:

- Booking register with 300 ready rows
- Guest turnover board with cleaner assignments and readiness checks
